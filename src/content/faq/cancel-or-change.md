Free until the cutoff for your bake date. After that the ingredients are bought
and the tray is scheduled, so a change of mind is not refunded. Anything wrong
with the food itself is made right within 48 hours with a photograph. The whole
policy, which is still a draft, is on the [refunds page](/legal/refunds).
