Inside California, yes. Outside California, no, and that is the law rather than
a preference: a cottage food operation may only sell within its own state.
There is no exception for a gift, and an order with an out of state address is
refunded rather than sent.

Shipping is by carrier and the cost is worked out at checkout from where it is
going.
