Yes, and that is said plainly at the foot of every page on this site and on
every label: {homeKitchenStatement}

A Class {operationClass} Cottage Food Operation is a category in California law
for food made at home and sold directly to the person eating it. The county
registers the kitchen and sets what may be made in it. It is not the same as a
restaurant inspection, and this site does not pretend otherwise.
